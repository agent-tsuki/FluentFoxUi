# Generated kanji course data

Do not edit by hand. Built by `scripts/kanji/build.py` from:

- **KANJIDIC2** (meanings, readings, grade, frequency) and **JMdict** (example
  words), © the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group,
  [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html).
- **KanjiVG** (stroke counts and parts), CC BY-SA 3.0.
- JLPT N5/N4 lists from [davidluzgouveia/kanji-data](https://github.com/davidluzgouveia/kanji-data) (MIT).
- Units, groups, key readings, reading tips, memory hints and look-alikes are
  authored in `scripts/kanji/authored/<level>.json` (memory hints are drafts
  until reviewed).

`<level>.index.json` is the light `KanjiLevelIndex` (Course, Browse, search);
`<level>.detail.json` is the full `KanjiLevelDetail` (kanji page, lessons, quizzes).
These files are derived works under CC BY-SA 4.0; the app shows the credit line
on kanji screens and PDFs.
